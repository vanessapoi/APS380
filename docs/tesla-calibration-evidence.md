# Tesla calibration: evidence and limits

The clean fourth drive verifies the configured simulator's equations. It is
not independent real-car data and cannot establish a correct battery capacity,
efficiency map or regenerative-control law. Its pack power and SOC were
generated by those same assumed parameters. No parameters have been fitted
to this drive, and the captured profile remains unchanged.

## Primary-source checks

| Parameter or behavior | Evidence | Remaining qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3D1 motor rating | [Tesla motor specifications](https://www.tesla.cn/ownersmanual/2017_2023_model3/en_cn/GUID-E414862C-CFA1-4A0B-9548-BE21C32CAA58.html) | Rating does not establish exact year/trim installation or a complete torque/efficiency map. |
| Single-speed reduction and nominal Li-ion pack voltage | [Tesla subsystems](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/2017_2023_model3/en_us/GUID-E414862C-CFA1-4A0B-9548-BE21C32CAA58.html) | Family-level manual; pack voltage in service varies with SOC and load. |
| 235/45R18 tire option | [Tesla replacement tire information](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Model3/ServiceManual/en-au/air/GUID-92AB1F42-7358-45AD-BACC-90A41BB3E715.html) | Supported for the 2017–2023 Model 3 family. Original fit depends on region and vehicle. Does not validate friction, stiffness or loaded rolling radius. |
| Lift-off regeneration | [Tesla braking and stopping](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/2017_2023_model3/en_us/GUID-3DFFB071-C0F6-474D-8A45-17BE1A006365.html) | Tesla describes dependence on battery state and temperature. Our constant-efficiency simplified control is not an OEM map. |

The working 50 kWh capacity, efficiencies, lift-off torque and regeneration
power limit remain assumptions. A fixed-voltage current estimate is distinct
from a voltage/current measurement or a modeled electrochemical cell current.
The profile's 1,611 kg curb mass also needs a confirmed year-specific source.

## Repeatable analysis

From the repository root:

```powershell
.\aps380_torcs_starter\.venv\Scripts\python.exe aps380_torcs_starter\validate_ev_drive.py
```

Results are in `aps380_torcs_starter/output/tesla_drive_04_validation/`:

- `audit.json`: source/profile/analysis hashes, equation checks, torque and
  power limits, nominal tire/gearing diagnostic, and calibration-readiness gaps.
- `phases.csv`: contiguous control phases with interval energy totals.
- `parameter_sensitivity.csv`: explicitly chosen one-at-a-time scenarios,
  holding the recorded shaft-work trace fixed. They are not confidence bounds.
- `validation.png` and `report.md`: plots and interpretation.

`scripts/process_tesla.ps1` now freezes the CSV, effective car XML and captured
profile before running both the pack analysis and drive audit. It refuses to
process a source that changes during copying. This keeps both reports tied to
the same samples and settings.

## What the fourth drive establishes

The energy counters close to numerical precision. Configured torque and power
caps are respected. No contact flags occur after the initial 0.1 s startup;
final damage is zero. Nominal gearing/radius is broadly consistent with motor
speed. However, the longest unsmoothed near-steady interval is only 0.4 s under
the audit's stated definition, so the recording is not a sustained cruise test.

Next, collect repeatable straight-line simulator runs with 10-second holds at
30 and 50 km/h, known SOC, and gentle lift-off/braking sections. For actual
vehicle calibration, match these against independent measurements from the
same Tesla variant, including pack voltage/current and road grade. Independent
coastdown/road-load data is needed to separate drag, rolling loss and slope.
Until those data exist, the external-calibration gate stays pending rather
than being passed by tuning against the simulator's own output.
